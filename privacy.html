Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary: Peek does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your tasks are stored only on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AEA YAZILIM E-TİCARET AMBALAJ PAZARLAMA LİMİTED ŞİRKETİ ("AEA Software", "we", "our", "us") handles information when you use the Peek mobile application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Your tasks

The tasks you create (titles, completion status, "Top 3" flags, colors, shapes, repeat days, reminder times and timestamps) are stored only on your device, in storage shared between the App and its widgets (an App Group container). They are not sent to our servers or to any third party. If you set reminders, the App asks for permission to send notifications; they are scheduled locally on your device and no push server is involved.

2.2 Analytics, accounts and tracking

Peek has no user accounts. The App contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

2.3 Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. The App uses Apple's StoreKit on your device to determine whether Peek Pro is active.

3. How We Use Information

Information that stays on your device is used only to show your tasks in the App and in the widgets, and to unlock Peek Pro features you have purchased.

4. Third-Party Services

Peek relies only on Apple services (StoreKit and WidgetKit). Apple's handling of data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete tasks or delete the App. Deleting the App removes its stored data. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is none for us to access, correct or export. If you are in the EEA, UK or Turkey (KVKK), you may contact us at any time with questions about your rights.

7. Children's Privacy

Peek does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Security

Your tasks are kept in the App's sandboxed storage on your device and protected by your device's security (passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and device encryption). Note that Lock Screen widgets can display your task titles while the device is locked; you can remove the widget or change Lock Screen visibility in iOS Settings if you do not want this.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date below will change when we do.
