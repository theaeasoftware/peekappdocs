What Peek does

Simple by design. Fast by default.

Lock Screen widgets

Rectangular, circular and inline widgets show your tasks the moment you pick up your iPhone.

Check off a task right from the widget. No need to unlock or open the app.

⭐

Top 3 for today

Home Screen widgets

Small and medium widgets for your Home Screen, too.

8 languages

English, Türkçe, Español, Deutsch, Italiano, العربية, Русский and Français.

Private by default

No account. No tracking. Your tasks stay on your device.

Free, with optional Peek Pro

Peek is free for up to 7 open tasks. Peek Pro unlocks unlimited tasks and is offered as a yearly subscription (with a 3-day free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage them in your Apple ID settings. See the EULA, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
